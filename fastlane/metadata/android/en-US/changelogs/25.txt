Paused apps get a way out, plus text on rows and launches that follow the icon.

New:
* When another app opens a paused app, the system dialog now offers Unpause and open instead of a dead end. Apps paused by an earlier version get that button on their own.
* Notification text on rows: the newest notification shows under the app name in favorites. Off by default.
* Apps open out of their icon and shrink back into it on a gesture back.

Fixed and tuned:
* The A-Z strip setting measures the whole reach from the screen edge and defaults to 40 dp instead of 56, so it no longer covers the leftmost icons.
* Shizuku is recognised by its API rather than by a package name, so Shizuku-Next, Shevery and other compatible managers work too.
* Importing settings is bounded (1 MB per file, 64 KB per string, capped lists, range-checked numbers), so a hand-edited backup can no longer crash the app.
* Copying a crash log replaces packages from other profiles with [private].

All 8 languages complete.
