Search, tags and locks.

New:
* Search finds apps by pinyin — initials and full spelling — with the match highlighted.
* App tags group apps across the alphabet: sections on top, three letters in the strip, multi-select tagging with existing tags offered.
* The Frequent tag follows usage on its own and can be switched off, taking its tags back.
* A per-app lock asks for the screen lock, with a lock badge on the name.
* The long-press menu gains uninstall; hiding an app asks first.
* Night dark theme, wallpaper blur, pinch-to-resize icons, reduced motion.

Fixed and tuned:
* Favorites reach the bottom edge like the app list and scroll fully into view; scrubbing past Z stays at Z.
* The strip rests in the lower half (full height sideways); the palette picker is a five-column grid.
* Settings back navigation returns to the section it came from; settings search finds every row.
* Paused apps grey out live; widgets refresh when their app freezes and unfreezes and are never cleaned away.
* Backups now cover every setting including the language; private-space keys stay out.
* About leads with the app name; tapping it opens the project page; the changelog follows the language.
* A hand-applied Frequent tag is refused; unlocking lifts a pause where the system allows.
