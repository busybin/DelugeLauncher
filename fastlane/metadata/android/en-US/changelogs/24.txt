The largest update since the fork: freeze apps, a new settings screen, and backups that cover everything.

New:
* Freeze apps with root or Shizuku: Stop, Suspend, Disable or Hide, one tab per method, each with its own advantage and drawback. The screen reports which root (Magisk, KernelSU, APatch) or Shizuku is answering.
* Freezing has triggers, not a switch: Freeze all, the corner button, a shortcut on the launcher's own icon, or automatic freezing a while after the screen goes off. The automatic run leaves the app on screen, apps with notifications and a phone on a charger alone.
* System apps stay out of the freeze list unless you turn them on; select all and invert fill or empty the list in one go, and both long-press menus freeze or unfreeze a single app.
* Settings is a two-column grid, with the search box still over every row.
* Export settings now covers every setting, the language, and re-pins the shortcuts it names. Freeze state and update-check records stay on the device.
* Distinct icons for each direction: a snowflake to freeze and a sun to unfreeze, a closed lock to lock and an open shackle to unlock. Uninstall is red in both menus.

Fixed and tuned:
* Widgets refresh when their app freezes or unfreezes, and a frozen widget is never cleaned away.
* Freeze commands are batched through one shell, so a long list no longer waits a command at a time.
* Reconcile clears a frozen mark the system no longer reports, instead of leaving an app greyed out forever.

All 8 languages complete.
