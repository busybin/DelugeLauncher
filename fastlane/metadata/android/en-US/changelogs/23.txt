Update check, smoother settings, and fixes.

New:
* A folder that expands near the bottom scrolls its apps into view instead of leaving the last rows off-screen.
* Settings -> About can check the release page for a newer version. It reads, and sends nothing.

Fixed and tuned:
* Edit layout: the grab handle sits clear of the system back gesture, so a reorder drag starts on a gesture-navigated phone, and a drag the system interrupts keeps the order you dragged it to.
* The A-Z strip: the letter under your finger is the one that lights, and letters hidden by the corner button leave a gap instead of squeezing the rest away from your finger.
* The clock, weather and agenda widget's settings open at once: apps, calendar and battery are read off the main thread, once, not every frame.
* Pausing or resuming an app no longer empties the icon cache; backups leave out the icon cache and the notification counts.
* Settings sliders follow your finger without writing to storage on every frame.
* The flashlight switch keeps its state after you leave the home screen, and the clock widget no longer asks for a 30-minute idle wake-up it never uses.
* All 11 languages complete.
