First release of Deluge Launcher, a fork of Victoria Launcher.

New:
* Blue Material 3 theme and a new app icon.
* The app can be given its own language, with ten translations plus Traditional Chinese.
* The A-Z strip follows the list while scrolling and lights up every letter on screen.
* Folder rows show how many apps they hold and whether they are open.
* A changelog in Settings, and a card offering to set Deluge as the home app.

Fixed and tuned:
* Opening the launcher is much faster: icons are cached on disk.
* Scrolling the app list is never stolen by a slightly-angled swipe.
* Returning to the home screen no longer takes seconds to draw, or goes blank.
* Widget height follows the widget; dead widgets from a backup are cleaned up.
* The status bar no longer sits on top of the first row when it is shown.
* Restoring a settings file re-pins the shortcuts it names.
