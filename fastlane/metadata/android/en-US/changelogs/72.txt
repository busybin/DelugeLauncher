New:
* Shortcuts pinned from a browser's "Add to Home screen" now land in Deluge. They can be renamed, given an icon, hidden, foldered or used for a quick launch, contributed by lennygatorsandghosts.
* Settings open on a list of sections instead of one long page.

Fixed:
* Pulling down for notifications works again over a widget that has nothing to scroll; only a widget that can scroll now takes the gesture.
* An A-Z strip set to always show no longer sits on top of the home screen content.
* The settings header is shorter, so it fits beside a camera cutout in longer languages.
